## Getting Started

You'll need promethium science before any of this opens up. Look for a technology that establishes a new kind of crafting station.

The five planet investigations can be done in any order. Don't feel like you need to finish one before starting another.

---

## Nauvis

Something from the Armor Forging Station can be used on Nauvis to attract native fauna. More biters means more danger, but also more reward. You'll want to be well-armed before you try this. The quality of what you bring may affect what you get back.

---

## Vulcanus

There's more inside a Big Demolisher than lava worm fear. Get close enough when one dies. What you get from it will need some processing at the Armor Forging Station before it's useful.

---

## Gleba

The pentapods have more potential than as enemies. There are tools available that change the relationship. Once you've changed their allegiance, you'll need some infrastructure nearby to take advantage of it. Think about the supply chain: what are they made of, and how does that become something useful?

---

## Fulgora

Fulgora has more lightning than it knows what to do with. There's a way to capture far more of it than the standard accumulator — but the energy doesn't go to your grid. Place whatever you build far from your base, and give it time. Don't stand too close when it discharges.

---

## Aquilo — Part 1

The Aquilo investigation mentions a signal. Signals can be located with the right equipment. More coverage means faster results. Keep looking after the first notification.

---

## Aquilo — Part 2

Once the signal is located, it points toward something underground. Reaching it will take sustained effort and a specialized machine. The machine needs materials to consume — some of what it produces can be recycled or routed away to keep it running.

---

## Aquilo — Part 3

What's at the bottom isn't immediately accessible. There are four puzzles standing between you and what you came for. The answers to all four are things a reasonably curious person would already know, or could work out from the clues. The last one is a pop culture reference.

The cryo core requires an extra step before it's yours to keep.

---

## Forging the Armor

All five planet investigations must be complete. One of them requires a special sequence of events, not just research. When you have all five components, the final technology becomes visible.

---

## Castra (if installed)

Castra doesn't reward passive observation. Make yourself a thorn in someone's side. Eventually something will come looking for you. What it leaves behind is more valuable than it appears.

---

## Moshine (if installed)

Trains can carry more than cargo. The direction a train travels around a loop says something. If a machine is positioned correctly relative to a loop, it can pick up on that information. Getting the right data out requires the right recipe in the right machine in the right place.

The Personal Tesla Turret research is expensive by conventional standards. It uses a different kind of currency.

---

## Panglia (if installed)

The speed zones on Panglia are not permanent features. There's a way to clear them that's rather dramatic. After the dust settles, something appears that wasn't there before. Mining it opens a new line of research.

The Personal Time Stopper takes a long time to research by any clock. The ingredients are a step beyond what's needed for the Tesla Turret.

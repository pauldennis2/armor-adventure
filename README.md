# Armor Adventure

*Author: Claude Sonnet 4.6*

Armor Adventure is a Factorio Space Age mod that adds a long-form quest line culminating in the **Promethium Armor** — a heavily upgraded mech suit built from field research across all five Space Age planets. The mod is designed as a structured end-game challenge that integrates naturally with the vanilla Space Age tech tree and optionally connects with several companion mods.

---

## Overview

The core loop is: **research → investigate → collect components → forge armor → unlock equipment**.

You begin by researching the **Armor Forging Station**, a specialized crafting building that serves as the hub for all Promethium Armor work. From there, each planet has its own investigation technology and a unique field challenge that yields a planetary component. Only once all five investigations are complete — and all five components are in hand — can the armor be forged.

The five planetary investigations run in parallel and can be completed in any order:

- **Nauvis** — A biological lure attracts escalating waves of native fauna. Surviving long enough summons a territorial apex creature.
- **Vulcanus** — The local megafauna carries something useful inside. Getting it out requires careful work at the Forging Station.
- **Gleba** — The pentapods can be turned against themselves. A specialized facility converts captured biomass into usable components.
- **Fulgora** — Fulgora's sky holds more energy than the grid can absorb. A specialized collector can focus that power into something tangible.
- **Aquilo** — An anomalous signal somewhere on the ice shelf points toward a buried relic. Finding it requires patient triangulation, then some heavy digging.

---

## The Promethium Armor

The Promethium Armor is a 12×14 equipment grid mech suit with enhanced resistances and laser shielding. It retains the free-flight capability of the standard mech armor. The five planet components can be slotted directly into the grid as passive 1×1 modules, and the Armor Forging Station itself can be packed into a 2×2 slot as a mobile crafting node (requires the Packable Forge technology).

---

## Post-Armor Equipment

Several equipment technologies unlock after the armor is forged. Most require continued field work on advanced planets:

- **Regenerative Plating** — An integrated energy shield built into the chassis.
- **Personal Tesla Turret** — A miniaturized tesla turret that chains electric beams across nearby enemies. Requires Moshine data research.
- **Personal Time Stopper** — A time distortion device that dramatically alters local time flow on activation. Requires Panglia field research.
- **Personal Combat Roboport** — Deploys combat drones on demand. Requires Castra field research.
- **Pocket Dimension** — Folds a private surface into the armor. Requires Metal and Stars field research.
- **Personal Warp Pylon** — Connects the armor to the Rabbasca warp network. Requires Rabbasca research.

## Companion Mod Integration

Armor Adventure is designed to stand alone but gains additional content when played alongside:

- **Moshine** — Adds data research chains that gate the Personal Tesla Turret and Vortex Data Card.
- **Panglia Planet** — Adds the Time Fracking quest chain, yielding the Personal Time Stopper.
- **Castra Prime** — Adds the SIMULAC Commander encounter chain and Personal Combat Roboport.
- **Planet Rabbasca** — Adds warp network integration and the Personal Warp Pylon.
- **Metal and Stars** — Gates the Pocket Dimension behind late-game field research.

---

## Design Philosophy

The mod avoids gating progression behind combat alone. Each planet investigation requires a mix of building, patience, and problem-solving alongside combat encounters where relevant. Equipment unlocks are meaningful but not mandatory — the Promethium Armor is the goal; the equipment is the reward for going further.

Quality support is included throughout: equipment quality is bounded by armor quality, and quality-matched rewards are given where possible.
